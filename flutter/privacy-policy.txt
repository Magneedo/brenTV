Privacy Policy

brenTV stores the provider addresses and credentials you enter, app settings,
favorites, viewing history, playback positions, and recording/download jobs
locally on your device. Recordings and downloaded media are saved in the
configured folders. Diagnostic logs and temporary files are stored locally.

Importing a catalog, watching or recording a stream, downloading media, and
loading program listings contact the providers or XMLTV addresses you configure.
Those requests may include the provider credentials and playback headers needed
for access. Those services have their own privacy policies.

brenTV has no app-operated account service or analytics backend and does not
upload your local library or viewing history to an app-operated service.
Opening an external link, such as Report issue, uses your browser and contacts
that website.

You can remove sources and manage saved media in the app. Uninstalling the app
may leave its local database, logs, and saved media on the device; remove those
files separately if you want to erase them.
